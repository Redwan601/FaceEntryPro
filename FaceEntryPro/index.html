Why departments switch

Built for the two things registers can't do: prove identity, and see risk coming.

Every capability below maps to a specific failure of manual registers and card-swipe systems — proxy marking, spoofing, and reports that only explain the past.

Trimodal recognition fusion

Facial geometry, iris pattern and voice-print are combined rather than checked alone, closing the gaps any single modality leaves open.

98.7% target accuracy

3D liveness detection

Depth-sensing distinguishes a real, present student from a printed photo, a screen replay, or a silicone mask before access is ever granted.

Anti-spoofing layer

Edge inference, on-device

A TensorFlow Lite model runs locally on a Raspberry Pi 4 at every entry point, so recognition keeps working through network outages.

No biometric data leaves the device

Predictive at-risk analytics

Attendance trajectories are correlated against academic performance to flag disengagement 3–4 weeks before it becomes a dropout statistic.

Early-warning, not a retrospective report

Spatiotemporal anomaly detection

Tailgating, badge sharing and irregular entry timing are surfaced in real time on a role-based dashboard, not discovered after the fact.

Live campus intelligence

GDPR-aligned by design

Because recognition never touches a public network, institutions meet data-privacy mandates without adding a separate compliance layer.

What happens in the 0.94 seconds after a student walks in.

01

Capture & fuse

Camera, iris sensor and mic capture simultaneously; the CNN fuses all three signals on-device.

02

Verify liveness

Depth sensing confirms a live, present person before any match is accepted.

03

Log & correlate

Attendance is logged instantly and cross-referenced against each student's academic record.

04

Alert & intervene

Faculty receive a risk alert weeks ahead, with enough runway to actually intervene.
